Enlil is my head, my face is the day,
Uraš, the perfect god, is the guardian of my face,
my neck is the necklace of Gula,
my arms are the crook of Sîn and Amurru,
my fingers are tamarisk, the bone of godhead,
they let no spittle come near my body.
Lugaledinna and Latarak are my breast,
my knees are Muḫra, my feet that walk abroad are the whole flock of heaven.
Whoever thou art, evil god, whom the warlock and the witch have sent to slay me:
art thou awake, come not hither,
art thou asleep, rise not up!
Let thy words be rotten apples, before god and king let them crumble.
At my door I have set Lugalirra, the strong god, and the messenger of the gods, Papsukkal:
"Smite my warlock and my witch upon the cheek,
turn back her word into her mouth!"
