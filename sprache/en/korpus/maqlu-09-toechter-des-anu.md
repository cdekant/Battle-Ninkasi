Two are they, the daughters of Anu in heaven,
three are they, the daughters of Anu in heaven.
They hold the rope and come down to me from heaven.
"Wherefore are ye risen up, whither go ye?"
"To seek the sorcerer and the sorceress of this man are we come,
to gather up their leavings,
to sweep together their refuse,
to kindle the brazier in the night are we come."
