Sent am I, and I go, charged am I, and I speak,
against my warlock and my witch hath Asalluḫi sent me, the lord of exorcism.
Ye of heaven, give heed, ye of the earth, hearken,
ye of the river, give heed to me, ye of the dry land, hear my mouth!
The howling wind is spellbound, blow not!
He of the staff and the pole is spellbound, blow not!
Let the road, the daughter of the great gods, stand still,
until I have spoken the word against my warlock and my witch.
The ox looseth, the sheep looseth:
let their word be loosed, but let my word not be loosed!
While I speak my word, let their word not stand before my word,
at the word that Asalluḫi hath spoken, the lord of exorcism.
