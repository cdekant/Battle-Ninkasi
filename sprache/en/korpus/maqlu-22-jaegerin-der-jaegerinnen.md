Huntress of huntresses,
witch of witches,
whose net is cast in the lanes,
whose eyes rove over the square of the city:
she ever seeketh the young men of the city,
among the young men of the city she seeketh me,
she ever peereth after the maidens of the city,
among the maidens of the city she peereth after me.
But I seek against thee the cult singers and the ecstatics, I break thy knot.
Let the warlocks bewitch thee, I break thy knot,
let the witches bewitch thee, I break thy knot,
let the snake charmers bewitch thee, I break thy knot.
I smite thee upon the cheek, I tear out thy tongue,
I fill thine eyes with spittle,
I make thine arms slack,
and thyself I make to rot,
and all that thou hast ever wrought I turn back upon thy head!
