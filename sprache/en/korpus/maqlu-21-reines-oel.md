Pure oil, clear oil, shining oil,
oil that cleanseth the body of the gods,
oil that sootheth the sinews of mankind,
oil of the spell of Ea, oil of the spell of Asalluḫi!
I smear thee with the oil of easing
that Ea gave for easing,
I anoint thee with the oil of life,
I lay upon thee the spell of Ea, the lord of Eridu.
I drive out the wasting, the jaundice and the chills of thy body,
I lift dumbness, torpor and misery from thy frame,
I ease the sick sinews of thy limbs.
At the word of Ea, the king of the Deep,
by the spell of Ea, by the incantation of Asalluḫi,
by the soft bandage of Gula,
by the easing hands of Nintinugga
and of Ningirima, the mistress of incantation:
Ea hath laid upon him the spell of the word of healing,
let the seven sages of Eridu soothe his body.
