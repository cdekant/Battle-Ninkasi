Away, away, begone, begone,
depart, depart, flee, flee,
go, go off, away and begone!
Let your wickedness go up like smoke to heaven!
From my body away, from my body begone,
from my body depart, from my body flee,
from my body go, from my body go off!
To my body turn not back,
unto my body draw not nigh,
to my body come not near!
By the life of Šamaš, the honoured, be ye adjured,
by the life of Ea, lord of the springs, be ye adjured,
by the life of Asalluḫi, the exorcist of the gods, be ye adjured,
by the life of Girra, who burneth you, be ye adjured!
From my body shall ye be parted!
