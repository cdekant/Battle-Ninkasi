When the witch crossed the river,
my sorceress plunged down,
she that stirreth up the sorcery against me sat at the ford
and hath swallowed the quay,
ever she seeketh me to pierce me.
The sages of the Deep saw her,
with wise learning, the art of Ea, they spake her ruin.
Ea, the king of the Deep, darkened her face,
he overwhelmed her with the falling sickness, confusion and trembling.
Her terror grew beyond measure,
the river cast her own fear upon her.
Over her images my hands are washed,
with mountain herb and pure juniper,
with soapwort that cleanseth mankind my hands are washed,
pure am I become and stand upon her witchcraft.
Let her witchcraft fill the steppe,
let the north wind bear her words away,
and all that thou hast wrought, let the wind bear it away!
