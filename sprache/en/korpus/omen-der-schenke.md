If a man spill beer at his left side: he shall not know the word of his god, his judgement shall draw nigh unto him.
That it draw not nigh: at the place where it befell, he poureth out threefold beer and water of the river, and it draweth not nigh.
If a man spill beer at his right side: a guest shall come into his house and tarry long.
If a man break the cup from which he drinketh: for three days grief shall befall him.
If a man let the jar fall and the jar break not: his house shall stand, his debt shall be forgiven him.

If in the ditch of the city a flood with beer be seen: the wives of men shall be wroth and slay their husbands with the weapon.
If from the well of the city beer be drawn instead of water: the prince shall rejoice, but the harvest shall be small.
If barley rain upon the city: the brewsters shall grow rich, and the price of bread shall rise.
If alewives be many in the city: the people shall be scattered, the market price shall fall.
If there be no alewife in the city: the gods of the city shall forsake it.

If a man enter the tavern regularly: for one year his heart shall be glad, thereafter he shall not be able to count his debts.
If a man sing in the tavern and the alewife laugh not: he shall be slandered before the king.
If a man sleep in the tavern until morning: his god shall seek him and not find him.
If a man loose his belt in the tavern: his wife shall know it ere he come home.
If a man make water at the door of the tavern: he shall not prosper.
That it draw not nigh: he spattereth beer right and left upon the doorposts of the tavern, and it draweth not nigh.

If a fly fall into a man's beer: he shall gain possessions.
If two flies fall into a man's beer: he shall share his possessions with his brother.
If seven flies fall into a man's beer: the gods have smelled the savour, and he shall soon be with them.
If the foam stand upon the beer: the brewster is beloved of Ninkasi.
If the foam upon the beer vanish at once: the word that the man speaketh over this beer shall not stand.

If the vat begin to ferment of itself in the night: Ninkasi is entered into the house.
If the vat ferment and the beer turn sour: an evil spirit dwelleth in the cellar.
That it draw not nigh: he poureth the sour beer upon the threshold, sweepeth the cellar with tamarisk and giveth Ninkasi a jar of the first brew, and it draweth not nigh.
If the vat foam over: the master of the house shall drink richly and keep nothing.
If the vat stay still and ferment not: the brewster hath forgotten the song of Ninkasi.
If the malt sprout upon the floor like grass: the year shall be good, the children of the house shall thrive.
If the malt grow mouldy upon the floor: sickness shall come into the house.
If upon the kiln the smoke go up to heaven: the gods shall accept the offering of the house.

If a man be drunken and praise his mother: he shall live long.
If a man be drunken and revile his god: in the morning his head shall burn like fire.
If a man be drunken and talk with the wall: a secret shall be revealed unto him, but he shall forget it.
If a man be drunken and find not the way home: Ištar shall show him the way.
If a man be drunken and sleep upon the threshold: the dead shall speak with him.
If a man be sober and yet stagger: a spell is laid upon him.
That it draw not nigh: he drinketh beer mixed with water of the river, speaketh seven times the name of Ninkasi, and it draweth not nigh.
If a man fast and drink beer: this man is pure.
