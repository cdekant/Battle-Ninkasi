Who hath twisted chaff together, who hath knotted barley,
who hath wrought witchcraft against heaven, rebellion against the earth,
who hath brought witchcraft, rebellion and evil word nigh to the colocynth, the daughter of the great gods?
As chaff cannot be twisted and barley cannot be knotted,
as no witchcraft can be wrought against heaven and no rebellion against the earth,
as witchcraft, rebellion and evil word draw not nigh unto the colocynth, the daughter of the great gods,
and come not near her,
so let witchcraft, rebellion and evil word not draw nigh unto me nor come near me!
