Born was she of waters that never stand still,
suckled by Ninḫursaĝa, who asketh not nor judgeth,
Ninkasi, born was she of waters that never stand still,
suckled by Ninḫursaĝa, who asketh not nor judgeth.

Thy city cleaveth to the ground, comb upon comb, full of honey and of flies,
there were feasts made for thee, till no man stood upright,
Ninkasi, thy city cleaveth to the ground, comb upon comb, full of honey and of flies,
there were feasts made for thee, till no man stood upright.

Thy mother is Nintil, lady of the deep, who never cometh up,
thy father is Enki, lord Nudimmud, who hath tasted all things already,
Ninkasi, thy mother is Nintil, lady of the deep, who never cometh up,
thy father is Enki, lord Nudimmud, who hath tasted all things already.

Thy dough, that riseth like a man at break of day, thou kneadest with the mighty shovel,
it smelleth of honey and of sourness, and thou workest it till it yieldeth,
Ninkasi, thy dough, that riseth like a man at break of day, thou kneadest with the mighty shovel,
it smelleth of honey and of sourness, and thou workest it till it yieldeth.

Thy sourdough thou pushest into the mighty oven,
there lie the loaves in rows like sheaves of gunida emmer before the hail cometh,
Ninkasi, thy sourdough thou pushest into the mighty oven,
there lie the loaves in rows like sheaves of gunida emmer before the hail cometh.

Thy malt lieth in the water and putteth forth shoots,
it writheth and twisteth like worms upon a bed that no one hath made fresh,
Ninkasi, thy malt lieth in the water and putteth forth shoots,
it writheth and twisteth like worms upon a bed that no one hath made fresh.

Thy mash thou stirrest in the vat,
it riseth and falleth, riseth and falleth like the bosom of a woman who shall find no sleep this night,
Ninkasi, thy mash thou stirrest in the vat,
it riseth and falleth, riseth and falleth like the bosom of a woman who shall find no sleep this night.

Thy spent grain thou spreadest upon the mighty mat,
it groweth cool and still like a god who hath at last been given what he lusted after,
Ninkasi, thy spent grain thou spreadest upon the mighty mat,
it groweth cool and still like a god who hath at last been given what he lusted after.

Thy great dry beer thou holdest with both hands,
honey and wine are therein and agree better than thy guests,
Ninkasi, thy great dry beer thou holdest with both hands,
honey and wine are therein and agree better than thy guests.

What ... (here someone hath spilt beer over the tablet)
... is the dry beer that she reached for,
Ninkasi, what ...
... is the dry beer that she reached for.

The fermenting vessel with the pierced bottom gurgleth and groaneth,
thou settest it upon the fat belly of the pithos, where it belongeth,
Ninkasi, the fermenting vessel with the pierced bottom gurgleth and groaneth,
thou settest it upon the fat belly of the pithos, where it belongeth.

Thy beer runneth down through the sieve into the pithos,
it cometh like Euphrates and Tigris in the time of the thaw, and no one hath built dams,
Ninkasi, thy beer runneth down through the sieve into the pithos,
it cometh like Euphrates and Tigris in the time of the thaw, and no one hath built dams.
