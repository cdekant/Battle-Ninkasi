Blazing Girra, firstborn of Anu,
thou art he that speaketh judgement, the hidden speech,
thou makest the darkness bright,
thou settest straight what is tangled and troubled,
to the great gods thou givest decision,
without thee no god passeth a verdict,
thou art he that giveth instruction and counsel.
The evildoer thou alone catchest in haste,
the wicked and the foe thou overcomest in haste.
I, the son of my god, am touched by witchcraft, therefore I stand before thee,
before god, king and lord I am made hateful, therefore I come unto thee,
whoso looketh upon me, to him I am grown loathsome, therefore I bow before thee.

Great Girra, pure god,
now before thy great godhead
two images of the warlock and the witch of bronze have I made by thy power.
Before thee I cross them, unto thee I hand them over:
let them die, but let me live,
let them be driven off, but let me go straight,
let them come to an end, but let me increase,
let them grow weak, but let me grow strong!
Splendid Girra, first among the gods,
vanquisher of the wicked and the foe, vanquish them, that no wrong be done me!
I, thy servant, will live and be whole and stand before thee:
thou art my god, thou art my lord,
thou art my judge, thou art my helper,
thou art my avenger!
