My friend is a witch, but I am a looser of spells,
the witch bewitcheth, but I am a looser of spells,
the witch is of Elam, but I am a looser of spells,
the witch is of Gutium, but I am a looser of spells,
the witch is a Sutean, but I am a looser of spells,
the witch is a snake charmer, but I am a looser of spells,
the witch is an ecstatic, but I am a looser of spells,
the witch is a smith, but I am a looser of spells,
the witch is a daughter of my city, but I am a looser of spells.
I have sent to the setting of the sun, they have gathered me their images,
the images of the seven and seven witches I hand over to Girra,
in the blazing kiln I burn them.
Girra, burn my warlock and my witch,
Girra, scorch my warlock and my witch!
Girra, burn them, Girra, scorch them,
Girra, vanquish them, Girra, consume them,
Girra, confound them!
Warlock and witch, sorcerer and sorceress,
let them go into the drain,
but let me pass over them like the water of the flood!
