I lift the torch and burn their images,
the images of the utukku, the šēdu, the lurker, the ghost of the dead,
of Lamaštu, of fever, of jaundice,
of the lilû, the lilītu, the ardat-lilî
and of every evil that seizeth mankind.
Melt, run and drip away!
Let your smoke go up to heaven,
let the sun put out your embers,
let the son of Ea, the exorcist, cut off your terror!
