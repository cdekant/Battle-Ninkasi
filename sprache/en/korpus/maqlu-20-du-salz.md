Thou, salt, that camest to be in a pure place,
for the food of the great gods hath Enlil appointed thee,
without thee no meal is made ready in Ekur,
without thee god, king, noble and prince smell no incense.
I am the man whom witchcraft holdeth captive,
whom the schemes seize as a scab.
Loose my witchcraft, salt, scatter my spittle,
take the schemes from me, then will I praise thee as the god that made me!
