Ye waters that go over all the lands,
that cross to and fro over all the mountains,
that shatter the quays and break the boat,
flowing water of the river, water of the Tigris and the Euphrates,
water of the sea, of the wide ocean:
the sages of the Deep have drawn you,
the seven sages of Eridu have adjured you,
through their pure spell ye are become pure and cool.
As through their pure spell ye are become pure and cool,
so let the heart of my warlock and my witch become pure and cool!
At the word of Ea, the king of the Deep,
I sprinkle your heart with water of the river,
I sprinkle your embers and your smoke with water of the sea,
at the word of Ea, Šamaš and Marduk and of the princess Bēlet-ilī.
