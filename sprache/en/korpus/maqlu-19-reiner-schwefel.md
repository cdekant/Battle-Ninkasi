Pure sulphur, the daughter of the great heaven, am I,
Anu created me,
Enlil and Ninlil brought me down into the land.
Sorceress, where couldst thou bewitch me?
Enchantress, where couldst thou enchant me?
As far as my head reacheth, heaven reacheth,
as far as my feet reach, the earth reacheth.
Upon the inner hem of my garment
is laid the spell of the sage of the gods, of Marduk.
