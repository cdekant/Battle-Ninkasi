Sherd of the lanes, why art thou ever at feud with me?
Why come thy messages ever unto me?
Witch, thy words, sherd upon sherd,
why do they reach me again and again to seize me?
I go up upon the roof and cover thy window,
I go down to the earth and block thy track,
in thy track I set a lurker,
a ghost of the dead that hunteth thee I make to seize thy road.
I smite thy skull and confound thy mind,
I trouble thy heart, that thou forgettest thine own flesh.
Sorceress, and thou that stirrest up the sorcery,
I am heaven, thou canst not befoul me,
I am the earth, thou canst not enchant me,
I am the thorn of the thornbush, thou canst not tread upon me,
I am the sting of the scorpion, thou canst not lay hold on me,
I am a high mountain, thy witchcraft, thy spittle,
thy bonds, thy evil schemes
draw not nigh unto me and come not near me!
