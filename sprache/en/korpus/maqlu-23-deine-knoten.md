Thy tightly knotted knots,
thy evil works, thy hostile schemes,
thy messages of evil
hath Asalluḫi, the exorcist of the gods, loosed and let the water bear away.
Let thine evil mouth be full of earth,
let thine evil tongue be bound with a gag,
at the word of Enbilulu, the lord of life.
