Witch, murderess,
slanderer, naršindatu,
exorcist, ecstatic,
snake charmer, agugiltu,
votary, nadītu,
servant of Ištar, kulmašītu,
huntress of the night,
spy of all the day,
that befouleth heaven,
that defileth the earth,
that bindeth the mouth of the gods,
that fettereth the knees of the goddesses,
that slayeth the young men,
that spareth no woman,
the assailant, the mutterer,
into whose witchcraft and spittle none may enter:
now they have seen thee, they have seized thee,
they have changed thee, they have turned thee about,
the words of thy witchcraft have they reversed,
Ea and Marduk have given thee over to Girra, the warrior.
Let Girra, the warrior, break thy knot,
and all that thou hast wrought, let him turn against thyself and make it wind!
