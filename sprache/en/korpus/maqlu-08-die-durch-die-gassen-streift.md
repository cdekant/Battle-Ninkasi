The witch that roameth the lanes,
that ever breaketh into the houses,
that creepeth through the alleys,
that spieth about the squares,
she turneth about, before and behind,
she standeth in the lane and turneth the feet of folk,
in the square she cutteth off the trade.
From the fair youth she robbed his manhood,
from the fair maiden she bore off her charm,
with her evil stare she took away her grace.
She looked upon the youth and robbed him of his guardian spirit,
she looked upon the maiden and bore off her charm.
The witch hath seen me and is come after me,
with her venom she hath cut off the trade,
with her spittle she hath cut off my gain,
my god and my goddess hath she driven from my body.

From the clay pit I have now pinched off clay for my witch,
the image of my sorceress have I shaped:
"Tallow I lay in thy belly that undoeth thee,
cornel wood I thrust into thy kidneys that burneth thee,
let the cornel wood that burneth thee cut off thy venom!
Above the city I have laid fire,
beneath the city I have cast embers,
into the house thou enterest I have cast fire.
Thou hast bewitched me, so let Girra devour thee,
thou hast had me bewitched, so let Girra vanquish thee,
thou hast plotted against me, so let Girra slay thee,
thou hast had others plot, so let Girra burn thee!
Let Girra, thy destroyer, make thee take the road of no return,
let raging Girra set thy body aflame!"
