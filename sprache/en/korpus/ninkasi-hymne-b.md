From running water art thou come forth,
Ninḫursaĝa tended thee like a nurse who knoweth too much,
Ninkasi, from running water art thou come forth,
Ninḫursaĝa tended thee like a nurse who knoweth too much.

Upon wax hast thou founded thy city,
and its walls thou didst finish before the sun could soften them,
Ninkasi, upon wax hast thou founded thy city,
and its walls thou didst finish before the sun could soften them.

Thy begetter is Enki, lord Nudimmud, who was never sober,
she who bore thee is Ninti, queen of the Abzu, who loveth the sweet and suffereth the salt,
Ninkasi, thy begetter is Enki, lord Nudimmud, who was never sober,
she who bore thee is Ninti, queen of the Abzu, who loveth the sweet and suffereth the salt.

Thou takest hold of the dough with the great shovel,
in the pit thou minglest the beer bread with sweet spices as a harlot her ointments,
Ninkasi, thou takest hold of the dough with the great shovel,
in the pit thou minglest the beer bread with sweet spices as a harlot her ointments.

Thou bakest the beer bread in the great oven till it crackleth,
thou heapest the husked grain into piles as high as the tombs of kings,
Ninkasi, thou bakest the beer bread in the great oven till it crackleth,
thou heapest the husked grain into piles as high as the tombs of kings.

Thou waterest the malt that resteth beneath the earth,
and noble dogs keep watch over it and bite even the princes who creep near,
Ninkasi, thou waterest the malt that resteth beneath the earth,
and noble dogs keep watch over it and bite even the princes who creep near.

Thou steepest the malt in the jar,
and the waves rise and fall like the rumour of a widow,
Ninkasi, thou steepest the malt in the jar,
and the waves rise and fall like the rumour of a widow.

Thou spreadest the cooked mash upon great reed mats,
and coolness lieth over it like the shroud over one who hath drunk too much,
Ninkasi, thou spreadest the cooked mash upon great reed mats,
and coolness lieth over it like the shroud over one who hath drunk too much.

With both hands thou holdest the great sweet wort,
thou brewest it with honey and wine, and neither cometh out unscathed,
Ninkasi, with both hands thou holdest the great sweet wort,
thou brewest it with honey and wine, and neither cometh out unscathed.

Thou ... the sweet wort into the vessel,
the rest hath time drunk up,
Ninkasi, thou ... the sweet wort into the vessel,
the rest hath time drunk up.

The fermenting vessel, that soundeth as sweetly as a woman behind a thin wall,
thou settest fittingly upon the great collector vat,
Ninkasi, the fermenting vessel, that soundeth as sweetly as a woman behind a thin wall,
thou settest fittingly upon the great collector vat.

When thou pourest the filtered beer out of the collector vat,
it rusheth forth like Tigris and Euphrates, and whoso standeth in the way is drowned,
Ninkasi, when thou pourest the filtered beer out of the collector vat,
it rusheth forth like Tigris and Euphrates, and whoso standeth in the way is drowned.
